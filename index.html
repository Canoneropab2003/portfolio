About Me

OPEN

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from the University of Bohol (2026), with hands-on experience spanning IoT-based systems, web applications, and classroom automation. My thesis, KlasEco, paired custom software with microcontrollers to automate real classroom equipment it placed 2nd at Create@UB 2026, which was the first real test of whether an idea on paper still held up once it had to run in front of judges.

Before that, I did my OJT at BukoLabs.io, working on a project called HapsayHub. That's where most of my web development habits actually came from less classroom theory, more building something a team could use day to day. Between the thesis and the OJT, I ended up with a fairly even mix of hardware and software experience, backed by a handful of certifications along the way.

On top of that, I've spent real time in customer-facing and administrative work clear communication, accurate data entry, and the patience to work through a problem methodically instead of guessing. That combination is what I'm looking to put to use next: development, IT support, systems or QA work, or anything computer-related where being able to both build the thing and explain it to someone else actually matters. Outside of work, I spend a fair amount of time on photography and design, which is probably obvious from how much attention this page gets.
